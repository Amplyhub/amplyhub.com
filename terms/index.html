Amply Terms of Use

Version 2026-11-07 (7 November 2026) Applies to drivers using the Amply mobile application in Nigeria.

When we publish a new version, you will be asked to accept it in the app before you can continue.

Amply is operated by AMPLYHUB, a company registered in France, whose registered office is at 57 rue d'Alleray, 75015 Paris, France. Its full details are in section 13. If you use the app, these terms are the agreement between you and us. Read section 4 in particular: it explains what Amps are and, just as importantly, what they are not.

1. What Amply is

Amply is a listening app for ride-hail and taxi drivers. You listen to music, audio content and short advertisements while you drive, and you accumulate a unit called Amps for the time you spend listening.

Amply is currently in a beta. That means the service is being tested with a limited number of drivers, features may change or stop working, and some of what is described below applies only when the full service launches. Every place where that is the case is marked.

Using Amply does not make you an employee, a worker or an agent of AMPLYHUB. You choose whether, when and how long you listen.

2. Who can use it

You must be at least 18 years old and hold a valid Nigerian mobile number.

One account per person and per phone number. During the closed beta our team opens your account and gives you your sign in in person. Sign up in the app is closed during the closed beta. When it opens, you will enter your phone number, we will send a code by SMS to check it, and you will then choose a password and give your age and platform. Creating more than one account, or using someone else's, is grounds for suspension and for the loss of the Amps in the accounts concerned.

A referral code is optional. If another driver gave you their code, you can enter it when you sign up in the app, or give it to our team when they open your account.

3. Your account

During the closed beta our team opens your account with your name and phone number, and gives you a temporary password in person. It works for 7 days. The first time you sign in you choose your own password, then you accept these terms and the privacy policy. Nothing is credited before that. When sign up in the app opens, you will enter your phone number, receive a code by SMS, then choose your password. After that you sign in with your password, or with your fingerprint or face if your phone supports it.

If you forget your password during the closed beta, write to hello@amplyhub.com or ask your Amply contact. Our team can give you a new temporary password. Resetting a password by SMS is switched off in the app for the closed beta: the sign in screen tells you to write to this address, and the Amply team will reset your password.

Your password is yours to protect. We will never ask you for it. Anyone who signs in with it is treated as you.

We hold your first name and your phone number. You may also give your last name, your age and the platform you drive for. When you sign up in the app, your age (between 18 and 80) and your platform are required to open the account. You can change them later in your profile. None of them changes what you earn.

4. Amps, and what they are not

Read this section carefully.

An Amp is a promotional loyalty unit issued by AMPLYHUB. It is not money, not a currency, not a deposit, not a security, not electronic money, and not a claim of any kind against AMPLYHUB or against anyone else. It cannot be bought. It exists only inside Amply and only for the purpose of obtaining the rewards we make available.

During the beta, Amps have no monetary value. The conversion rules will be published before they open.

During the beta there is no withdrawal and no in-app redemption. Your balance grows, your full history is visible to you, and rewards are handled by our team outside the app, case by case. We will tell you how, and we may change how.

4.1 How you earn

  • Listening. About 171 Amps an hour of active listening.
  • The Boost. Your first hours of credited listening earn twice the normal rate. During the free beta you have 50 hours of Boost, or 52 hours if you joined with a referral code.
  • No sign up bonus. No Amps are credited when you sign up or when our team opens your account.
  • Referral. While the referral rewards are switched on, and they are switched on whenever listening is credited, a driver who joins with your code earns you two things. First, 3 percent of the Amps they earn from listening, from the day they become active. Second, 2 hours of Boost, once for that driver, when they are active and have had at least 1 hour of listening credited in all, up to 20 hours of Boost in total. A driver becomes active once they have had listening credited on 3 different Lagos days. Their listening before that day is not shared, and nothing is paid for a time when the rewards were switched off. These rewards are paid by us. They are never taken from what the other driver earns.
  • Partner surveys. Not available during the beta.

No reward in Amply depends on chance. What you earn depends only on the rules in this section.

4.2 What limits your earnings

  • Five hours a day. You are credited for at most 5 hours of listening per day, counted on Lagos time. Audio keeps playing after that. You simply stop earning until the next day.
  • The presence check. After an hour of continuous listening the app asks you to confirm that you are still there. If you do not answer, crediting pauses until you interact with the app again. This exists so that a phone left playing in an empty car does not earn like a working driver.
  • Location. This version of the app does not collect your location. Section 1.3 of the privacy policy says what happens if a future version needs it.
  • Late reports. If you listen without a network, the app keeps your listening and sends it, in the order you listened, when it reconnects. It is credited to the Lagos day you actually listened. Listening reported more than 7 days late is not credited. Neither is a listening that reaches us after a later one from the same account, for example from a second phone or from a phone whose clock is wrong.
  • A pause for everyone. During the beta we may pause the crediting of Amps for every driver at once, for example to stop a fault or an abuse. The app says so when it happens. Listening during a pause is not credited, not even later. Amps already credited stay credited.

These limits are applied by our server automatically. You can ask a person on our team to look again at any of them for your account (privacy policy, section 2.2).

4.3 Freezing, corrections and loss

  • After 30 days without listening your Amps are frozen. For an account that has never listened, the 30 days count from the day it was opened. Frozen Amps are not lost. Listening again restores them, unless our team froze them after a review. In that case you can ask us why.
  • We may correct a balance when it is wrong, in either direction. Every correction is recorded with its reason and the name of the person who made it, and you can ask us for that reason.
  • We may suspend an account and cancel its Amps where we find duplicate accounts, automated or simulated listening, or any attempt to earn without actually listening.

4.4 What we may change

We may change earning rates, limits, the Boost and the rewards on offer. Changes that reduce what you earn take effect no earlier than 14 days after we tell you, and never retroactively: Amps already credited stay credited. This notice does not apply to a pause for everyone described in section 4.2.

5. The audio

The music, podcasts, jingles, news and advertisements in Amply belong to their rights holders and are licensed to us. You may listen to them personally through the app. You may not record, copy, redistribute, broadcast or extract them.

Advertisements cannot be skipped. Non-sponsored content can. That is the deal that funds what you earn.

The programme is the same for every listener and is chosen by us. You cannot choose what plays, and there is no guarantee that any given piece of content will be available.

6. What the app costs you

Access to Amply is free during the beta. Any future price will be announced in advance and will apply only after you have accepted it. No payment and no card are asked of you.

Your mobile data is your own cost. The app downloads audio, and how much depends on how you use it. Where an unmetered network is available the app can download in advance so that listening costs you nothing in data.

7. What you agree not to do

  • Run the app on an emulator, a modified device, or a modified version of the app.
  • Automate, simulate or fake listening by any means.
  • Hold more than one account.
  • Sell, transfer or trade your account, your referral code or your Amps.
  • Attempt to reach parts of our systems that are not yours.

8. Suspension and closing your account

You may close your account at any time, from the app, with your password. You are signed out everywhere at once. For 90 days you can sign in and ask, in the app, to restore your account. Signing in alone does not restore it. At the end of the 90 days your account is anonymised and any positive balance of Amps is lost. A negative balance is not cancelled. The Amps ledger is kept without your name or phone number, as the privacy policy describes.

If you cannot open the app, write to hello@amplyhub.com and we will close your account in the same way.

We may suspend or close an account that breaches these terms. Where it is reasonable to do so we will tell you why and give you a chance to answer. Where we find fraud we may act immediately.

We work to keep Amply running, and we do not promise it will always be available. Content, features, reward availability and rates can change. We are not responsible for your mobile network, your phone, or the cost of your data plan.

Do not use the app in a way that distracts you from driving. Set your programme before you drive. You are responsible for your own driving and for complying with Nigerian road traffic law.
